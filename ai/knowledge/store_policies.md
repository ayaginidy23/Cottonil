# Cottonil Shopping Assistant - Knowledge Base (DEMO)

> Sample policy text written for the HVIA Task 3 demo. It is NOT the official Cottonil
> policy. Payment methods, channels and size ranges below come from the project data.

## Returns and exchanges
Items can be returned or exchanged within 14 days of purchase with the receipt or the
order number. Items must be unused, with the original tags and packaging. Underwear,
socks and swimwear can only be returned if the packaging is still sealed, for hygiene
reasons. Online orders can be returned at any branch or by courier pickup. Refunds go
back to the original payment method (card reversal, wallet or cash). An exchange for a
different size or colour is free of charge.

## Refund timing
Cash refunds are given immediately at the branch. Card reversals usually take 7 to 14
working days to appear, depending on the bank. Wallet refunds usually arrive within
24 to 48 hours.

## Delivery and shipping
Online orders are delivered to all governorates by in-house delivery, a local courier
or an express carrier. Most orders arrive within 1 to 4 days; remote governorates can
take longer. A shipping fee is added at checkout depending on the area and carrier.
You can track your order in this chat with your order number.

## Payment methods
Accepted payment methods are cash, card (POS), mobile wallet, cash on delivery for
online orders, and bank transfer (mainly for wholesale and reseller orders).

## Size guide
Adult sizes go from M and L up to XL, 2XL, 3XL and 4XL. Some items such as socks and
accessories come in one free size. Kids items use age sizes 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7 (years).
If you are between two sizes, pick the bigger one for a relaxed fit, especially for
cotton items which can shrink slightly after the first wash.

## Product care
Cotton items: wash at 30-40 C with similar colours, do not bleach, tumble dry low or
dry in the shade, iron at medium temperature. Wash new dark items separately the first
time.

## Wholesale and reseller program
Registered resellers (B2B customers) can buy at wholesale conditions through the
wholesale channel and pay by bank transfer. Resellers can ask this assistant for their
purchase history, their most-ordered products and reorder suggestions. For new reseller
accounts, contact the wholesale team through any branch.

## Branches
Cottonil has retail branches and reseller points of sale in many governorates. Ask the
assistant for the branches in your governorate to get names, cities and addresses.

## Contact and complaints
For complaints about an order, share the order number in this chat. The assistant can
check its status, delivery and returns. For anything else the assistant cannot solve,
a branch team member or customer service can help.
